# CityPress 0.2.0-beta.5

## English

Themed editions, professional newspaper copy, named transit service evidence and corrected monthly fiscal balance.

- News, economy, transport, public services and commentary can have separate themed editions. Material decides the themes and length; no mandatory filler. News and opinion have explicit genre labels. The game offers a page directory and previous/next navigation; Word starts each themed edition on a new page. Legacy single-edition issues remain compatible.
- Stock editorial instructions upgrade automatically. Technical collection warnings stay out of articles; unsupported conclusions are omitted, not rewritten as certainty. Process/disclaimer phrases are rejected before caching. Important stories can be accompanied by grounded analysis and commentary without invented interviews or achieved effects.
- Passenger lines are checked independently after each one-game-minute interval, in bounded batches. Real names, modes, distinct stopping points, fares and actual service vehicle evidence enter editorial facts. Baseline routes and ordinary service gaps cannot be called new openings; confirmed opening stories must retain the line name.
- Fixed a beta.4 fiscal bug: native expense-array entries are positive. Monthly balance is revenue minus expenses, not their sum. Old incorrect balance values/highs are excluded from new editorial input, while original journals/issues remain intact. A corrected metric starts a fresh high-water baseline.

Verified locally: Release build, core/cache/finance/transit/old-issue compatibility, game-assembly callback harness, UI types/build/audit, browser page navigation at three scales, and three themed Word pages rendered and inspected. Native game/save acceptance and actual DeepSeek editorial output remain pending; no paid API requests were used for these tests.

Install the complete CityPress-0.2.0-beta.5.zip into local Mods/CityPress after exiting the game, then restart. Existing settings, keys and newspapers are preserved. Re-generate to apply new copy/editions; re-exporting an old issue preserves its old content. Generation remains manual by default.

## 简体中文

新增主题分版、专业日报措辞、具名线路运营采集，修正月度财政结余。

- 按素材组织要闻、经济、交通、民生、评论等版面，不强制凑版。消息、简讯、分析、评论标明体裁。游戏内支持版面目录与前后翻版，Word 按主题新起一页，兼容旧单版刊物。
- 旧默认编辑方针自动升级。正文只保留核实后的新闻事实，不抄写采集限制或模板式免责措辞；依据不足的结论舍弃，不包装成确定事实。重要新闻可配有具体论点的分析或评论，不虚构采访与成效。
- 客运线路独立分帧检查，完成后间隔一游戏分钟，采集真实线路名、交通方式、去重停靠点、票价与实际服务证据。既有线路基线和普通车辆间断不冒充新开通，确认的新线运营报道必须保留线路原名。
- 修复 beta.4 财政错误：原生支出数组为正数，结余应为收入减支出。旧错误结余及其突破不再送入新稿，日志和报纸原件保留；正确指标重建高位基线。

已完成本地 Release 编译、核心/缓存/财政/线路/旧刊兼容、实际程序集回调、UI 类型/构建/审计、三种缩放翻版，以及三版 Word 渲染检查。原生游戏/存档和真实 DeepSeek 成稿仍待实测，本次测试未调用付费 API。

退出游戏后完整安装 CityPress-0.2.0-beta.5.zip 到本地 Mods/CityPress，再重启。设置、密钥与旧报纸保留。新措辞与分版需重新生成，单纯重新导出旧刊不会改写正文。默认仍为手动生成。
